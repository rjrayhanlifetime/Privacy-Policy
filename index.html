Privacy Policy

My Overtime

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the My Overtime mobile application ("the App") handles information when you use it. Please read this policy before using the App.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter information such as working hours, overtime hours, dates, rates, and calculation details to use the App's features. The information handled by the App depends on the features and version you use. If the App stores entries on your device, those entries remain in the app's local storage unless you delete them or remove the App.

2. Collection and Use of Information

Information may be used to calculate, display, and manage your overtime records and to operate the App. This policy does not promise that no information is collected. Any analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, account, backup, or online-sync services included in the version you use may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Sharing of Information

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared where necessary to provide features you choose to use, operate third-party services integrated into the App, comply with applicable law, or protect the App and its users.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures appropriate to the App to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You should avoid entering sensitive information that is not needed to use the App.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may include or rely on third-party services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. Please review the policies of any third-party services shown in the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact the developer so the matter can be reviewed.

7. Your Choices

You can stop using the App at any time. You may be able to delete information stored locally through the App's features or by clearing its app data or uninstalling it. Deleting local app data may permanently remove records that have not been backed up.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App or its data practices change. The revised version will be published at this page with an updated date.
